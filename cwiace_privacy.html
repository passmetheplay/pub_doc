CWI Exam - Privacy Policy

Introduction

Thank you for using CWI Exam. We take privacy seriously. This policy explains what information the app handles and how it stays on your device.

Local data only

All data CWI Exam stores is kept locally on your device using Core Data and UserDefaults. Examples include study progress, quiz results, bookmarks, preferences, reminders, and similar app state. This data is not transmitted to our servers because we do not operate account-based cloud storage for CWI Exam.

No accounts

CWI Exam does not require you to create an account or sign in. There is no user profile hosted by us.

No personal data collection or transmission

We do not collect personal information through CWI Exam for our own use, and we do not transmit your study content or identity to our systems as part of normal app operation.

No ads or tracking SDKs

The app does not show third-party advertisements and does not integrate third-party analytics or advertising SDKs that track you across other companies' apps or websites.

In-app purchases

Optional purchases (such as CWI Exam Pro) are processed by Apple through StoreKit. Payment and subscription status are handled according to Apple's terms and privacy policy. We do not receive your full payment card details.

Data deletion

  • Uninstall: Removing CWI Exam from your device deletes locally stored app data as part of iOS app removal.
  • In-app reset: Where available, you can reset progress or settings inside the app without uninstalling.

Children's privacy

CWI Exam is designed for adults preparing for the Certified Welding Inspector (CWI) examination. It is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The updated version will be indicated by a revised "Last updated" date shown below.
