About ASVAB Prep

ASVAB Prep is a focused exam preparation tool for the Armed Services Vocational Aptitude Battery (ASVAB). With 677 practice questions across Verbal, Math, Science, and Technical domains, it provides comprehensive coverage to help you achieve the AFQT score needed for your military career goals.

The app is designed for self-paced study. All content is available offline once downloaded, so you can study anywhere. Progress is saved locally on your device and synced across sessions automatically.
