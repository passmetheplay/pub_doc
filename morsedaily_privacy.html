Morse Daily Privacy Policy

Summary: Morse Daily does not require an account, show ads, use tracking SDKs, or send your learning history to our servers.

Information stored on your device

Morse Daily stores learning progress, lesson results, practice history, sound preferences, onboarding state, and purchase entitlement cache locally on your device using Apple system storage such as SwiftData and UserDefaults.

No account or developer-operated cloud service

You do not need to create an account or sign in. We do not operate a server that receives or stores your learning history, answers, or preferences.

No advertising, analytics, or tracking

Morse Daily does not contain third-party advertising or analytics SDKs and does not track you across apps or websites.

In-app purchases

The optional Morse Daily Lifetime purchase is processed by Apple through StoreKit. Apple handles payment credentials and transaction processing under Apple's terms and privacy policy. We do not receive your full payment card details.

Permissions

The current version does not require an account, microphone, camera, contacts, location, or notification permission for its learning features. Sound is generated locally on the device.

Data deletion

Deleting Morse Daily from your device removes the app's locally stored learning data as part of the normal iOS app removal process. Apple may retain purchase history associated with your Apple Account so that eligible purchases can be restored.

Children's privacy

Morse Daily is a general-audience learning app. Because we do not collect personal information through the app, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The latest version and update date will always appear on this page.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to bringmemyfun@gmail.com.
