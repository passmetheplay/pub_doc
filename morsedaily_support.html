Frequently asked questions

I cannot hear the Morse tone

Check your media volume and audio output. In the app, open Me → Sound Settings, choose a comfortable pitch, and use Play Test Sound.

What is included for free?

Your Daily lesson and review of learned signals remain free. Morse Daily Lifetime unlocks unlimited extra learning sessions and lets you choose any new signal whenever you want.

How do I restore a purchase?

Open Me, select the Lifetime card, then choose Restore Purchase. Use the same Apple Account that made the original purchase.

Where is my progress stored?

Learning progress is stored locally on your device. Morse Daily does not require an account or upload your learning history to our servers.
