About CWI Exam

CWI Exam is a focused exam preparation tool for the AWS Certified Welding Inspector (CWI) certification exam. With 196 practice questions and 3 mock exams, it covers welding processes, inspection methods, codes & standards, metallurgy, and quality assurance — all the key areas tested on the CWI exam.

The app is designed for busy professionals. All content is available offline, so you can study during travel, between inspections, or at home. Progress is saved locally and persists across sessions.
