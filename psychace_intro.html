About PsychAce

PsychAce is an AP Psychology exam prep app that pairs comprehensive coverage with high-volume practice. Five units of content, detailed notes, and flashcards give you a single place to learn and review. More than five hundred MCQs support quick sessions, unit-based review, science-practice alignment, difficulty progression, bookmarks, and a dedicated mistakes loop.

Five mock exams simulate timing and length so pacing feels familiar before test day, with an estimated AP score to contextualize results. For writing, PsychAce includes the 2025–2026 FRQ formats—AAQ and EBQ—with self-scoring so you build rubric awareness as you draft. Readiness dashboards, weekly accuracy trends, and analytics across Science Practice (SP) and difficulty help you study deliberately. Reminders, streaks, and a countdown reinforce consistency, and PsychAce Pro adds full access to units, mocks, FRQs, richer analytics, and dark mode.
