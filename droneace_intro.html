About Drone Ace

Drone Ace is a focused exam preparation tool for the FAA Part 107 Remote Pilot Certificate. With 553 practice questions across 10 topic sections, it provides comprehensive coverage of all exam domains including regulations, airspace classifications, airport operations, weather fundamentals and effects, loading & performance, radio communications, emergency procedures, aeronautical decision-making, and Part 107 basics.

The timed mock exam mirrors the real FAA Part 107 test format — 60 questions in 120 minutes with a 70% passing threshold. Each question includes a detailed explanation to help you understand not just the correct answer, but the reasoning behind it.

All content is available offline once downloaded, so you can study anywhere — in the field, during breaks, or at home. Progress is saved locally on your device and synced across sessions automatically.
