About Auto Ace

Auto Ace is a focused exam preparation tool for ASE (Automotive Service Excellence) certification. With 1,657 practice questions across 9 exam areas, it provides comprehensive coverage of all ASE test series including A1 Engine Repair, A2 Auto Transmission, A3 Manual Drive Train, A4 Suspension & Steering, A5 Brakes, A6 Electrical Systems, A7 HVAC, A8 Engine Performance, and G1 Maintenance & Light Repair.

Timed mock exams mirror authentic ASE test formats with realistic question counts and time limits. Each question includes a detailed explanation to help you understand not just the correct answer, but the reasoning behind it.

All content is available offline once downloaded, so you can study anywhere — in the shop, during breaks, or at home. Progress is saved locally on your device and synced across sessions automatically.
