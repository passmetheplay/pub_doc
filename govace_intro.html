About AP Gov

AP Gov is built for AP U.S. Government & Politics students who want serious exam prep without clutter. The app mirrors the breadth of the course across five units, pairs concise study guides with flashcards and key terms, and backs it up with hundreds of multiple-choice questions and authentic FRQ formats.

Landmark SCOTUS cases and foundational documents live in a dedicated reference library so you can cite accurately and compare reasoning. Readiness tracking, daily stats, streaks, reminders, and a countdown help you stay consistent from the first week of school to exam day. AP Gov Pro unlocks unlimited practice, fuller FRQ scoring guidance, and richer analytics when you are ready to go deeper.
