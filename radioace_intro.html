About RadioAce

RadioAce is purpose-built for FCC commercial radio operator license exams. The app contains the complete, official question pool for all five exam elements—Element 1 (Marine Radio), Element 3 (General Radiotelephone), Element 7 (GMDSS), Element 7R (GMDSS Restricted), and Element 8 (Ship Radar)—so you study the exact questions you will see on test day.

Figures, circuit diagrams, and charts render inline so you can practice the visual questions that appear on the real exam. Realistic mock exams mirror the official format, and the built-in pass prediction tells you when you are ready.
