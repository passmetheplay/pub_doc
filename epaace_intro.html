About EPA 608 Ace

EPA 608 Ace is a focused exam preparation tool for the EPA Section 608 Refrigerant Handling Certification. With 1,194 practice questions across 4 certification types, it provides comprehensive coverage of all exam domains including refrigerant types, environmental impact, safety & handling, EPA regulations, recovery & reclamation, leak detection, and system repair.

The app supports all four certification levels: Type I (small appliances), Type II (high-pressure equipment), Type III (low-pressure equipment), and Universal (all types combined). Each mock exam mirrors the real test format with the same 70% passing threshold.

All content is available offline once downloaded, so you can study anywhere — on job sites, during breaks, or at home. Progress is saved locally on your device and synced across sessions automatically.
