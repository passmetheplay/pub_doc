About CitizenAce

CitizenAce is designed to help you pass the U.S. Citizenship (Naturalization) Test administered by USCIS. The app covers all 128 official civics questions from the 2025 test version, plus the 100-question 2008 legacy version for reference.

The app uses spaced repetition to help you retain information efficiently, and includes realistic mock exams that mirror the actual USCIS interview format — 10 questions with a pass threshold of 6 correct answers.

Whether you are a first-time applicant or qualifying under the 65/20 exemption, CitizenAce provides the tools you need to prepare with confidence.
