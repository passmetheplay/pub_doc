About CNA Exam

CNA Exam is a focused exam preparation tool for the Certified Nursing Assistant (CNA) competency exam. With 2,615 practice questions and 8 mock exams, it provides comprehensive coverage of all CNA exam domains including patient care, safety procedures, communication skills, infection control, and legal & ethical responsibilities.

The app is designed for self-paced study. All content is available offline once downloaded, so you can study anywhere — during clinical rotations, on breaks, or at home. Progress is saved locally on your device and synced across sessions automatically.
