ASVAB Prep - Privacy Policy

Introduction

Thank you for using ASVAB Prep. We take privacy seriously. This policy explains what information the app handles and how it stays on your device.

Local data only

All data ASVAB Prep stores is kept locally on your device using UserDefaults. Examples include study progress, quiz results, bookmarks, preferences, and similar app state. This data is not transmitted to our servers because we do not operate account-based cloud storage for ASVAB Prep.

No accounts

ASVAB Prep does not require you to create an account or sign in. There is no user profile hosted by us.

No personal data collection or transmission

We do not collect personal information through ASVAB Prep for our own use, and we do not transmit your study content or identity to our systems as part of normal app operation.

No ads or tracking SDKs

The app does not show third-party advertisements and does not integrate third-party analytics or advertising SDKs that track you across other companies' apps or websites.

In-app purchases

Optional purchases (such as ASVAB Prep Pro) are processed by Apple through StoreKit. Payment and subscription status are handled according to Apple's terms and privacy policy. We do not receive your full payment card details.

Data deletion

  • Uninstall: Removing ASVAB Prep from your device deletes locally stored app data as part of iOS app removal.
  • In-app reset: You can reset all progress and settings inside the app from the Me tab without uninstalling.

Children's privacy

ASVAB Prep is designed for adults and young adults preparing for the Armed Services Vocational Aptitude Battery (ASVAB) exam. The app does not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The updated version will be indicated by a revised "Last updated" date shown below.
